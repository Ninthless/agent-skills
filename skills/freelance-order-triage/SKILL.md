# Freelance Order Triage

## Core decision

Assess the actual deliverable, acceptance owner, inputs/access, deadline, budget model, revisions, codebase/domain risk, deployment, and ethics. Choose one posture: `take`, `clarify`, `paid discovery`, `stage`, `hourly`, `reshape`, or `decline`. Clear, information-complete, low-risk work may be accepted directly; do not manufacture uncertainty.

Use fixed price when scope, acceptance, inputs, deadline, and revisions are bounded. Use discovery, staged, or hourly work when unknown code, moving requirements, access, or production risk prevents a defensible commitment. Reshape dishonest student submission requests into legitimate tutoring, review, debugging, or attributable support.

## Adaptive output

Always include the posture, decisive evidence, and a client-ready reply. Add only modules needed for the decision:

- `compact`: straightforward low-risk accept/decline or one focused clarification
- `standard`: posture, key risks or scope boundary, necessary questions, client-ready reply
- `full`: complex rescue, regulated, payment, deployment, multi-party, ethics, or milestone work requiring hidden scope, acceptance, revisions, exclusions, and staged pricing logic

Do not force a fixed nine-section report. Ask only questions that change price, schedule, acceptance, or risk. Keep internal risk analysis separate from client wording.

Define included work, exclusions, assumptions, client inputs, acceptance checks, revision limits, and handoff only to the level necessary for a reliable engagement. Never promise third-party approval or results dependent on unavailable credentials, assets, or platform policy. Recommend domain research only when unfamiliar business rules materially block estimation.
