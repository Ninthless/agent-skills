# Evidence and Decision

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer official specifications and vendor or maintainer documentation. Use release notes for version changes, repository evidence for actual project constraints, and independent sources for operational experience when primary sources do not cover it.

## Freshness and compatibility

Record version, publication date, support status, runtime constraints, protocol level, and known incompatibilities when they affect the decision. Do not treat latest as automatically best.

## Option discipline

Compare only viable choices. Include keeping the current approach when it satisfies the constraints. Do not create alternatives to decorate a direct factual answer.

## Decision record

Separate evidence, inference, and recommendation. Explain decisive trade-offs and rejected options without pretending uncertainty is resolved.

## Bounded spike

Specify one question, a time or scope limit, synthetic or isolated inputs, observable success criteria, cleanup, and what decision the result changes. A spike must not create a production dependency or silently become the delivered implementation.
